2. Settings

Playing time is split as evenly as possible. If some players must sit an extra quarter, it's the lowest levels first (and whoever has played the most this season breaks ties). Level boost moves that many more quarters from the lowest-level players to the highest-level players (max 3 quarters each, min 1). Players are kept to their listed positions, usually play Q1+Q3 or Q2+Q4, keep the same position all game, and each quarter's total skill is balanced. Chemistry groups (their own tab) are kept on the field together, or apart, whenever possible.

Lineup

Change any cell with its dropdown. Red outline = not a listed position. Footer shows each quarter's position counts.

Edit by quarter

Drag a player onto another to swap positions, onto someone sitting to sub them in, or into "Sitting" to take them off. On a phone you can also tap one player, then tap another. 2q = quarters this game; counts update live.

Roster

Positions: tick every position a player can play (G = goalie, D = defense, M = mid, F = forward). Changes save automatically. Level: 1 (developing) to 5 (strongest). Aim for a spread; the balancing works best when not everyone is a 3.

Chemistry groups

Play together: players who click (e.g. a strong midfield trio) are put in the same quarters. Keep apart: spread players out (e.g. your two best, or two who don't mix). Click names to add or remove them.
